      <p>I've liked making games since childhood. Since 2011, I've built games with Flash and Unity, working with studios while continuing to explore ideas through my own projects.</p>

      <div class="article-section">
        <h2>Commercial experience</h2>
        <p>I began with social and iGaming projects, then worked on mobile games. These early roles taught me how to build for different audiences and support games after release.</p>
        <p>At Saber Interactive, I worked on UI integration for the Armored Warfare PS4 port. At iLogos, I supported development and releases for an Android 4X game, gaining experience with larger production teams.</p>
        <p>Moving to Unity at Fun-To-Mass, I helped turn rapid prototypes into released mobile games and led development as those projects grew.</p>
        <div class="portfolio-map article-experience-map" aria-label="Fun-To-Mass experience">
          {% include experience-fun-to-mass.html %}
        </div>
        <p>At Voodoo's GameOps Live Studios, I focused on established games: building new features, improving player experiences, and supporting reliable releases.</p>
        <div class="portfolio-map article-experience-map" aria-label="Voodoo experience">
          {% include experience-voodoo.html %}

        <p>Across these roles, I've combined hands-on development with an understanding of production and long-term game support. My <a href="{{ '/expertise/' | relative_url }}">technical background</a> covers Unity and C#, gameplay systems, UI, LiveOps, and release workflows. For detailed skills and employment history, see my <a href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1R72GYAeLcvy37-s4zTsqWBdSOEFc9MEM/view?usp=sharing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CV</a>.</p>

      <div class="article-section">
        <h2>Independent Projects</h2>
        <p>Outside commercial work, I make small games to experiment and learn. I've taken part in 20+ game jams, including 18 Ludum Dare Compo entries, exploring ideas without the constraints of a production schedule.</p>
        <p><a href="https://huindie.itch.io/punch" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PUNCH</a> started as a Ludum Dare 54 entry that ranked #1 in Fun. It grew into <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/4106530/PUNCH_FOREVER/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PUNCH FOREVER on Steam</a> for Windows and macOS, with browser and mobile versions too. You can <a href="https://huindie.itch.io/punch-forever" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">play PUNCH FOREVER</a> and explore my <a href="https://huindie.itch.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">other projects on itch.io</a>.</p>
        <p>In 2026, I'm expanding my experience and exploring how modern AI tools can support game development. I've registered the business name DenisK Interactive to give my independent and client work a clearer professional framework, from contracts to business administration.</p>
